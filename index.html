1585–1606: Roanoke Island & Coastal Encounters

When Ralph Lane's expedition landed on Roanoke Island in 1585, dense Algonquian, Iroquoian, and Muskogean nations thrived along the Atlantic coast and Piedmont. None of the 13 permanent colonies yet existed, though early coastal contact introduced pathogens along trade routes.
